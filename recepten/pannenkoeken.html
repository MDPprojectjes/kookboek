Erna's pannenkoeken

Ingredients

  • 2 apples
  • 200g flour
  • 100g sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 100ml milk
  • 100g butter

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 180°C.
  2. Peel and slice the apples into thin wedges.
  3. In a bowl, mix flour, sugar, baking powder, cinnamon.
  4. Add eggs, melted butter, and milk. Stir until smooth.
  5. Pour batter into a greased baking tin.
  6. Arrange apple slices on top in a nice pattern.
  7. Bake for 40–45 minutes until golden brown.
  8. Let it cool a bit before slicing. Enjoy!

📝 Notes

Gebruik genoeg appels als je dit voor mama maakt!
